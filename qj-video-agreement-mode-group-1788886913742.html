3. Term & Termination

This agreement commences on the date of signing and runs for a 90 days initial term for the marketing package. Videography is billed per shoot with a minimum commitment of one shoot.

Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice after the initial term. Quality Jobs reserves the right to terminate immediately in the event of non-payment exceeding 14 days.

4. Payment Terms

Invoices are issued at least 5 business days before each shoot date or the first of each calendar month (for management fees). Payment is due within 7 days of invoice date. A late fee of 1.5% per month applies to outstanding balances.

5. Client Obligations

The Client agrees to: provide timely access to premises and personnel for scheduled shoots; provide accurate business information; respond to communications within 2 business days; and make payments on time.

6. Intellectual Property

Upon full payment, all video and image content produced becomes the sole property of Mode Group. Quality Jobs retains the right to use content as case studies or testimonials unless the Client requests otherwise in writing.

7. Results Guarantee

7.1 Revenue Guarantee

Quality Jobs guarantees to generate $100,000 AUD in new revenue for Mode Group within 90 days of campaign launch. If this target is not met, Quality Jobs will continue delivering all marketing management services at no management fee until the guarantee is achieved.

7.2 Conditions

The guarantee applies to the marketing services (Section 2.3) only. The Client must be responsive, supply requested assets promptly, and maintain agreed ad spend for the guarantee to apply.
